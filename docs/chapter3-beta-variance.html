The variance depends on α and β through the number of imagined tosses α + β and the balance between them. More imagined tosses give a narrower band. With α = β the mean stays at 1/2 and α alone sets the width. The two variance readouts are computed two ways (E[r²] minus the squared mean, and the closed formula) and agree.
